Our Story

Hands‑on.
Heart‑first.

LITA Agency is a family-built, hands-on agency with over 20 years of experience representing people of all ages — babies, kids, teens, adults, and seniors — across modeling, acting, voiceovers, and extras.

What sets us apart is simple: we don't just submit and forget. We guide every talent through the entire process, from coaching self-tapes to polishing submissions, giving each person the best possible chance to shine.

We are honest, transparent, and deeply involved at every step — because your success is our success.

How It Works

From Application
to Opportunity

01
Apply & Submit

Reach out with your photos and basic info. No professional portfolio required — we work with beginners and veterans alike.

02
Review & Onboard

Our team personally reviews every application. If it's a fit, we onboard you and match your profile with relevant casting needs.

03
Casting Calls

When an opportunity matches, we send the brief, coach your self-tape, review your submission, and polish it before it goes out.

04
Booked & Supported

When you book a job, we're with you from contract to set. We celebrate your wins and keep building your career from there.
